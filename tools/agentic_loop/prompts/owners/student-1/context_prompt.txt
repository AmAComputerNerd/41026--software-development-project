Feature: Notifications

This service manages a student's notifications, their delivery
preferences, and AI-generated digests of that activity. Core
functionality:

- Notifications: create/read notifications with a StudentId, Type
  (Deadline/Grade/Automation/Account/AI), SourceMicroservice, Message,
  IsRead flag, and CreatedAtUtc; list/filter, mark a single notification
  or all notifications as read, delete a notification, and push new
  notifications in from other services.
- NotificationPreferences: create/read/update/delete a per-student
  preference keyed by NotificationType and NotificationChannel
  (InApp/Email), with an Enabled flag and UpdatedAtUtc, controlling
  whether/how a student receives notifications of that type.
- AiDigests: generate and list AI-produced summaries (Summary,
  GeneratedAtUtc) of a student's recent notifications, keyed by
  StudentId.

Out of scope for Release 0: authentication/user accounts, any UI,
cross-service or shared-database context (the service only knows about
notifications pushed to it, not the originating domain data), and any
MCP integration - digest generation calls an LLM directly, not via MCP.
